ingredients:
  - "310 g All purpose flour"
  - "150 g Salted Butter"
  - "1.75 tsp Baking Powder"
  - "150 g Cane Sugar"
  - "25 g Granulated Sugar"
  - "2 Medium Eggs"
  - "3 tbsp Mixed Spices"
  - "1.5 tsp Vanilla Extract"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven (175°)"
  - "Line 2 baking sheets"
  - "In a bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt. Set aside."
  - "In a seperate bowl, mix the eggs, vanilla extract, and butter until well combined."
  - "Gradually add the wet mixture to the dry mixture until a crumbly dough forms."
  - "Add mixed spices."
  - "Form the cookies and bake until they look good."
note: "Cookies expand alot in the oven!!"
